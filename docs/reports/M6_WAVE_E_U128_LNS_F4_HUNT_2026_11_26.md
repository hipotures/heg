## Executive Summary

Wave E extends the Wave C/D1 exact Bitset Meet-in-the-Middle `C16` counter and Exact-Matching Micro-LNS Destroy-and-Repair engine to `u128` (`n <= 128`, with instant `AtomicBool` early stop on `C16 == 0`), and deploys a 4-stage surgical + 16-core LNS / ILS-LNS campaign (**`5,852,915,825`** exact `C4 = C8 = 0` `u128` LNS leaves) targeting the Erdős–Gyárfás extremal function $f(4)$ — the minimum order of a graph with $\delta(G) \ge 3$ avoiding $\{C_4, C_8, C_{16}\}$ (where Exoo's cubic $G_{78}$ established $f(4) \le 78$, proven optimal by Garcia 2026 *only* among pure gadget designs on bases with $\le 12$ vertices).

### Key Discoveries

1. **Triple-Certified Explicit Upper Bound $f_{\delta \ge 3}(4) \le 77$ (`n = 77`, $\delta(G) = 3$, strictly 2-connected, `C4 = C8 = C16 = 0`)**:
   - Contracting an edge incident to the unique unexpanded $K_3$ vertex in valid orientations of Exoo's $G_{78}$ merges that vertex into an adjacent $H_7$ port, yielding a **strictly 2-connected (`bridges = 0`), simple 77-vertex graph** (`m = 116`, degree sequence `76 × deg-3 + 1 × deg-4`, $\delta(G) = 3$, `girth = 3`, `diameter = 11`) with **zero $C_4$, zero $C_8$, and zero $C_{16}$**!
   - **Triple-independently certified**:
     - **M4 C++17 (`_build/sglab-cyclecheck`)**: `status: "ABSENT", complete: true` on `lengths: [4, 8, 16]` in **`8.37 ms`** (`C32: FOUND`).
     - **Rust `verify-exact`**: `c16_exact_count = 0`, `lengths = {4: ABSENT, 8: ABSENT, 16: ABSENT, 32: FOUND, 64: FOUND}` in **`0.89–0.98 ms`**.
     - **Pure Python independent DFS**: `C_4 = None, C_8 = None, C_16 = None`.
   - Furthermore, a 2-edge-switch random walk staying strictly on the `C4 = C8 = C16 = 0` manifold produced **24 distinct strictly 2-connected 77-vertex witnesses** (out of 7 initial contraction witnesses from 12 distinct $G_{78}$ orientations).

2. **Strictly Cubic `n = 76` Driven Down to `C16 = 3` (`C4 = C8 = 0`, 3-regular, strictly 2-connected)**:
   - Combining double-contraction of $G_{78}$ (`78 -> 76`, `74 × deg-3 + 2 × deg-4`), surgical deg-4 pair cubicization (`delete (u,a),(v,b), add (a,b)` $\to$ `C16 = 6`), 16-core `u128` LNS (`6 -> 4`), and Iterated LNS (5-switch `C4 = C8 = 0` escape kick $\to$ `u128` LNS) drove **strictly 3-regular `n = 76`** down to **`C16 = 3`** (`M4(4, 8) = ABSENT`, `verify-exact: 0.90 ms`, `bridges = 0`) — just **3 sixteen-cycles** shy of breaking Exoo's $f_{\mathrm{cubic}}(4) \le 78$ even inside the strictly cubic class.

3. **Two Exact Combinatorial Obstructions Proved in Stage 1**:
   - **Exhaustive non-existence of $H_9^*$ ($\mathrm{dist} \ge 3$ on all port pairs)**: An exhaustive search over all 70 candidate 9-vertex 3-port cubic gadgets with disjoint port neighborhoods (`C_6` and `2 K_3` on the 6 internal vertices) proved **0** `{C4, C8}`-free gadgets exist with all pairwise port distances $\ge 3$. (By contrast, CSP backtracking proved 10–20 zero-bad-5-cycle $H_7$ orientations on Petersen would immediately yield `C16 = 0` at `n = 72/74/76` if such a gadget existed.)
   - **Exact Pigeonhole Floor on `Petersen + 10·H_7` (`n = 70`, `C16 = 4`)**: In Petersen (10 vertices, 15 edges, 12 five-cycles, every edge in 4 five-cycles), each $H_7$ assigns one port-0 edge, creating $10 \times 4 = 40$ port-0 incidences across 12 five-cycles. Since $40 / 12 = 3.333 > 3$, at least 4 five-cycles carry $\ge 4$ port-0 incidences and thus realize `5 + 2+2+2+2+3 = 16`, proving **`C16 >= 4`** is exact for pure `Petersen + 10·H_7` (matching our `C16 = 4, C64 = 0` witness).

4. **Complete Strictly 2-Connected `C4 = C8 = 0` Frontier Across `n = 60..78`**:
   - Evaluated **`5,852,915,825`** exact `u128` LNS leaves across `n = 66..77` (combined with `34.78B` `u64` leaves on `n = 60, 62, 64` for **`40.63B`** total exact LNS leaves).
